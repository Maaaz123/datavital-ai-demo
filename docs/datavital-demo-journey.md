# DataVital AI — 10-Minute Enterprise Demo Journey

**BUILD_ID context:** `v79-enterprise-integration`  
**Audience:** Executive / board / investor / pilot sponsor  
**Data:** Synthetic demonstration — not live hospital feeds

---

## Path

1. **Executive Overview** — open `/executive`. Scan ribbon (~30 seconds): occupancy, revenue, experience, safety, ED wait, contribution.  
2. **Identify a concern** — pick a Top Priority (e.g., bed pressure, denials, overdue CAPA, critical stockouts, constrained service lines).  
3. **Drill into the source domain** — follow the priority link (Beds, RCM, Quality, Supply Chain, Strategy, …).  
4. **Investigate with Vital Assist** — open Assist on the domain screen; review Observe / Investigate with period-scoped evidence.  
5. **Review source facts and limitations** — read disclosure (synthetic, grain rules, CM ≠ profit, reports ≠ harm, etc.).  
6. **Run an eligible What-if** — on the domain Optimization screen; change parameters; note illustrative / non-additive warnings.  
7. **Compare intervention options** — keep assumptions visible; do not sum overlapping opportunities.  
8. **Submit a human-governed decision** — Decide tab; AI does not auto-approve clinical/financial actions.  
9. **Identify the follow-up KPI** — use the opportunity’s follow-up metric.  
10. **Return to executive priorities** — `/executive` or `/command` for COO exceptions; confirm the same concern still links to the owning domain.

---

## Guardrails for the presenter

- Do not claim real-time telemetry.  
- Do not present what-if deltas as achieved ROI.  
- Do not treat Domains 12–15 as product-owner approved unless separately recorded.  
- Prefer one vertical story; avoid touring all 80 screens.
